# 07 — Q&A Bank (Easy → Expert)

Format: Q | short simple answer | source ids | tags | difficulty.

## Existence of God
1. Is there any proof God exists? → Yes: things that begin need a cause; the universe began; you didn't make yourself (Quran 52:35). | ARG-1, ARG-2 | easy
2. Who created God? → No one. Only things that BEGIN need a cause; God never began. | OBJ-6 | easy
3. Why can't the universe be the uncaused thing? → It's made of changing, dependent parts, so it looks contingent, not necessary. | ARG-3 | medium
4. What is the fitrah? → The inborn feeling that God is real (Quran 30:30; Bukhari 1358). | ARG-4 | easy
5. Isn't "God did it" just a gap-filler? → Only weak if it's a temporary gap; consciousness may be a deeper, in-principle gap. | ARG-6, OBJ-9 | hard
6. What's the kalam argument in one line? → The universe began, so it has a cause. | ARG-2 | easy
7. Doesn't quantum physics show things come from nothing? → No — a quantum vacuum is not "nothing"; it has fields and laws. | OBJ-11 | hard
8. What is a "necessary being"? → Something that must exist and depends on nothing. | glossary | medium
9. Is the Big Bang proof of God? → It fits a beginning, but don't over-claim "proof"; lean on the logic. | ARG-2 | medium
10. What's the design argument? → The universe's constants are finely set for life. | ARG-7 | medium
11. Doesn't the multiverse beat fine-tuning? → It's unobserved and just moves the question back. | OBJ-10 | hard
12. Why is there something rather than nothing? → Because a Necessary Being exists and gives being to all else. | ARG-3 | hard

## Problem of Evil / Suffering
13. If God is good, why is there evil? → Life is a test (67:2); free will; limited human wisdom; justice in the afterlife. | OBJ-1 | medium
14. Why natural disasters and disease? → We can't see all reasons; suffering can raise ranks and be repaid perfectly later. | OBJ-2 | hard
15. Why do babies and animals suffer? → Hardest case; Islam affirms justice for all and hope for children; limited wisdom + afterlife. | OBJ-3 | hard
16. Isn't "hidden reasons" just an excuse? → No — it's honest humility about our tiny knowledge, plus a positive afterlife story. | OBJ-2 | hard
17. Does suffering disprove God? → No; at most it raises a hard question, fully answered by test + wisdom + hereafter. | OBJ-1 | medium

## Morality
18. Can atheists be good? → Yes, they can ACT good; the question is whether atheism can GROUND real right and wrong. | OBJ-7 | easy
19. Why do we need God for morality? → Without God, "good" tends to become opinion or feeling, not objective. | ARG-5 | medium
20. What did Mackie admit? → That objective values would make God more probable (so he denied objective values). | ARG-5 | medium
21. Euthyphro: is good good because God says so? → False dilemma; good flows from God's perfectly good NATURE. | OBJ-4 | medium
22. Isn't "God's nature" circular? → No more than any bedrock explanation; it stops the regress at the best place. | OBJ-4 | hard
23. Can't science tell us right from wrong? → No — you can't get "ought" from "is" (is/ought problem). | OBJ-8 | medium

## Science / Consciousness
24. Doesn't science explain everything? → It explains HOW nature works, not WHY anything exists, or meaning, or consciousness. | OBJ-8 | medium
25. What's the "hard problem"? → Why we have inner felt experience at all (Chalmers, 1995). | ARG-6 | hard
26. Is Islam against science? → No; the Quran invites study of nature (3:190–191). | OBJ-8 | easy
27. Are there scientific miracles in the Quran? → Be careful; many scholars say don't lean on these as proofs. | 03 flags | medium

## Qadr / Free Will / Hell
28. If Allah decreed everything, am I free? → Yes; Allah's knowledge doesn't force you; you truly choose. | OBJ-12 | hard
29. Is it fair to be judged for a decreed life? → We don't know our decree and we feel/use real choice daily. | OBJ-12 | hard
30. Isn't eternal hell too much? → Judgment matches the person's true state and rejection; mercy is vast. | OBJ-13 | medium

## Why Islam / Prophethood / Miracles
31. Why Islam and not another religion? → Move to Quran + Prophet Muhammad's ﷺ evidence + pure tawhid. | OBJ-14 | medium
32. You only believe because you were born Muslim? → Origin doesn't decide truth (genetic fallacy); same applies to atheists. | OBJ-14 | medium
33. Why believe in miracles (Hume)? → Follow the evidence's quality; the Quran is a lasting, testable sign. | OBJ-15 | hard

## Meaning / Hiddenness / Doubts
34. Life has no meaning without God? → Islam gives real, given purpose (51:56); invented meaning is fragile. | OBJ-16 | medium
35. Why is God hidden? → He's not fully hidden — signs everywhere; and faith is a test. | OBJ-5 | hard
36. Is it wrong to have doubts? → No; doubts are normal. [IslamQA](https://islamqa.org/shafii/seekersguidance-shafii/169537/dealing-with-doubts-about-islam/?claude-citation-11004a75-a140-4d89-8cd6-334ed2d70a0d=70baed6c-a77f-4b4d-8c89-2a8c4a47203f) Seek knowledge and ask scholars (islamqa.org 169537). | 05 | easy
37. How do I stop waswas? → Seek refuge in Allah, say "Amantu billah," stop arguing with it, change activity. | 05 | easy

## Method / Adab
38. How should I start a dawah talk? → Be warm, ask questions, find the root of their doubt. | 05 | easy
39. What if I don't know the answer? → Say so honestly and offer to find it. | 05 | easy
40. Should I try to "win"? → No; aim to guide gently; guidance is from Allah (28:56). | 05 | easy
41. What's steelmanning? → Saying the other side's view in its strongest fair form first. | 04 | easy
42. Biggest young-da'i mistakes? → Arrogance, mocking, over-claiming, deep philosophy too early. | 05 | medium

## Logic
43. Valid vs sound? → Valid = right shape; sound = valid + true premises. | 04 | medium
44. What's a strawman? → Twisting the other side into a weak version. | 04 | easy
45. What's the is/ought fallacy? → Jumping from facts to values with no bridge. | 04 | medium
46. What's burden of proof? → Whoever claims must support it. | 04 | easy
47. What's the genetic fallacy? → Judging a belief by where it came from. | 04 | medium
48. What's a false dilemma? → Offering two options when more exist (like Euthyphro). | 04 | medium

## Harder / Expert
49. Define "begins to exist." → Comes into being after not existing (in time or with time). | ARG-2 | hard
50. Answer Rowe's fawn example. → "We see no reason" ≠ "there is no reason"; plus afterlife compensation. | OBJ-2 | hard
51. Answer Schellenberg's non-resistant non-believer. → Signs are available; test needs some hiddenness; hearts can block. | OBJ-5 | expert
52. Answer Oppy's eternal-universe reply. → Completed infinite past is problematic; brute eternal universe just stops asking. | OBJ-11 | expert
53. Is the contingency argument a composition fallacy? → No — the universe's parts are all dependent, so the whole is too. | ARG-3 | expert
54. Does divine command theory make morality arbitrary? → No — commands flow from God's necessary good nature. | OBJ-4 | hard
55. How is the Quran's "miracle" different from Hume's target? → It's a lasting, examinable challenge, not one-off testimony. | OBJ-15 | hard
56. What's skeptical theism? → The view that we can't expect to see all of God's reasons. | OBJ-2 | hard
57. Jabarites vs Qadarites? → Two extremes on qadr; mainstream is the middle path. | OBJ-12 | expert
58. Is "the universe is just there" (Russell) a good answer? → It's a conversation-stopper, not an explanation. | ARG-3 | hard
59. Can evolution "debunk" the fitrah? → Explaining how we got a belief doesn't show it's false. | ARG-4 | hard
60. What's the best SINGLE opener argument? → For beginners: "You didn't make yourself, and nothing makes itself" (52:35). | ARG-1 | easy
61. How to talk to someone grieving? → Empathy first, hope second; never cold logic. | P7/OBJ-3 | medium
62. When should I walk away from a debate? → When it's mockery/bad-faith; protect your peace and faith. | P10/05 | easy
