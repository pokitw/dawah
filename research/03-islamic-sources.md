## Key Quran Verses (reference + simple meaning)
- **52:35–36** — "Were they created by nothing, or were they the creators?" → We must have a Maker. (Main Quranic argument.)
- **30:30** — "…the fitrah of Allah upon which He created people…" → We're born knowing God.
- **67:2** — "[He] who created death and life to test you as to which of you is best in deed." → Life is a test. (Verified: Surah Al-Mulk, verse 2.)
- **51:56** — "I did not create jinn and mankind except to worship Me." → Our purpose.
- **16:125** — "Invite to the way of your Lord with wisdom and good instruction, and argue in the best way." → How to do dawah.
- **3:159** — "By the mercy of Allah you were gentle with them…" [Troid](https://www.troid.org/invite-to-the-way-of-your-lord-with-wisdom-and-fair-preaching-al-nahl-16125/?claude-citation-11004a75-a140-4d89-8cd6-334ed2d70a0d=69c51aeb-c937-41d4-a840-8bff2b03d60a) → Be gentle.
- **28:56** — "You do not guide whom you love, but Allah guides whom He wills." → Guidance is from Allah, not us.
- **41:34** — "Repel [evil] by that which is better…" → Answer harshness with kindness.
- **3:190–191** — Signs in the heavens and earth for people who think. → Islam invites reflection on nature.
- **41:53** — "We will show them Our signs… until it becomes clear that it is the truth." → Signs point to God.
- **4:82** — "Do they not reflect upon the Quran?" [DeenUp](https://www.deenup.app/blog/how-to-overcome-doubt-in-islam?claude-citation-11004a75-a140-4d89-8cd6-334ed2d70a0d=15b4f832-f0a3-4943-9bd5-fde62d032bf2) → Reflection is invited.

## Key Hadith (collection, number, grade) — verified on sunnah.com
- **"Who created your Lord?" (Shaytan's whisper):** Sahih al-Bukhari **3276**; Sahih Muslim **134**. Grade: agreed upon (muttafaqun 'alayhi). Wording: seek refuge in Allah and stop. URL: https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3276 , https://sunnah.com/muslim:134
- **"Amantu billah" response to waswas:** separate narration (Ibn al-Sunni via 'Aishah), graded Sahih by al-Albani in Sahih al-Jami' **6587**. (Do NOT attribute "Amantu billah" wording to Bukhari 3276.)
- **"Every child is born upon the fitrah…":** Sahih al-Bukhari **1358** (also 1385; Tafsir 4775); Sahih Muslim **2658**. Grade: agreed upon. URL: https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1358 , https://sunnah.com/muslim:2658
- **"Nothing repels the decree except dua…":** Sunan al-Tirmidhi **2139**; narrated by Salman al-Farisi; graded **hasan** by al-Albani. (Popular "change the decree" is a paraphrase of "repels the decree.") URL: https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:2139
- **"If Allah guides one man through you, that is better for you than red camels":** al-Bukhari and Muslim (cited in islamqa 26745). Encourages the da'i.

- al-Ghazali — *Tahafut al-Falasifa* (Incoherence of the Philosophers): infinite regress, universe has a beginning.
- Ibn Sina — *al-Najat*, *al-Isharat*: contingency / Proof of the Truthful.
- Ibn Taymiyyah — *Dar' Ta'arud al-'Aql wa'l-Naql*: reason and revelation don't truly conflict; fitrah.
- Ibn al-Qayyim — writings on divine wisdom and evil.
- al-Razi — *al-Matalib al-'Aliya* (advanced kalam).
- al-Sanusi — *Umm al-Barahin* (creed with rational proofs).

## Honesty Flags
- The "Amantu billah" wording is a SEPARATE narration from Bukhari 3276/Muslim 134 — don't merge them.
- Do NOT present "scientific miracles in the Quran" as knockout proofs — many scholars warn this is weak/risky.
- The exact science at the universe's first instant is debated — lean on philosophy for kalam.
