# 05 — How to Do Dawah (Adab) and Stay Safe

## 1) The Golden Verses
- **16:125** — Invite with (a) wisdom, (b) good preaching, (c) arguing in the BEST way. [Islam365](https://islam365.io/topic/dawah-preparing_dawah_conversations?claude-citation-11004a75-a140-4d89-8cd6-334ed2d70a0d=a40af005-8a84-4f20-953a-f5446b4f2247)
- **3:159** — Be gentle; harshness pushes people away. [Troid](https://www.troid.org/invite-to-the-way-of-your-lord-with-wisdom-and-fair-preaching-al-nahl-16125/?claude-citation-11004a75-a140-4d89-8cd6-334ed2d70a0d=309d8e1a-7659-4ee8-8473-97af70aee114)
- **28:56** — You cannot guide anyone; only Allah guides. So relax and be kind; results aren't on you.
- **41:34** — Answer bad with good; an enemy can become a close friend.

## 2) First: Find the ROOT of their doubt
Ask kindly. Three main roots:
1. **Intellectual doubt** — they want reasons/evidence. → Give arguments + sources (files 01–03).
2. **Bad experience / pain** — hurt by family, community, trauma. → Listen first. Don't debate a wound.
3. **No exposure** — they just never learned real Islam. → Share clearly and simply.
Matching the answer to the root is the whole game.

## 3) Ibn al-Qayyim's idea: people are different types
Some are seeking truth, some are proud, some are just hurt, some are playing games. Treat each differently. Wisdom (hikmah) = right words, right person, right time.

## 4) Emotional vs Intellectual doubters
- Emotional: comfort, presence, dua, patience. Fewer arguments.
- Intellectual: clear logic, honest sources, admit limits.
- Many people are BOTH. Watch their words and feelings.

## 5) What to say when you DON'T know
Say: "That's a great question. I don't know the full answer, but I'll find out / here's a scholar or paper that covers it." This is HONEST and STRONG. Guessing or making up a hadith is a sin and destroys trust.

## 6) Common mistakes of young du'at (avoid!)
- Arrogance / wanting to "win" instead of guide.
- Mocking atheists or their heroes (breaks 16:125).
- Over-claiming ("science PROVES the Quran," "this is 100% certain").
- Debating to show off in public.
- Talking about very deep philosophy before you're ready (can shake your own faith).
- Forgetting dua — guidance is from Allah (28:56).

## 7) Protecting YOUR OWN faith
- **Waswas (whispers):** intrusive doubts about God. The Prophet ﷺ said to seek refuge in Allah and STOP engaging (Bukhari 3276, Muslim 134). A separate narration: say "Amantu billah" (I believe in Allah). Then change what you're doing (stand up, make wudu, pray). [DeenUp](https://www.deenup.app/blog/how-to-overcome-doubt-in-islam?claude-citation-11004a75-a140-4d89-8cd6-334ed2d70a0d=ab56b55a-cb95-4f41-b37a-6152490291bc)
- **Don't argue with waswas in your head** — that feeds it.
- **Separate waswas from real questions:** waswas = repeating, shameful, no real trigger → seek refuge. Real question = wants evidence → study + ask scholars.
- **Cautions on deep philosophy (scholars):** al-Ghazali (in islamqa 88184) split philosophy into parts — logic/math are useful; some metaphysics can harm the weak. islamqa 99983: only those grounded in Quran and Sunnah should dive deep, so they can tell truth from error. So: build your basics FIRST, keep good company, keep worship strong, and take doubts to real scholars (islamqa.org 169537).
- **Keep a "faith routine":** daily Quran, dhikr, salah, good friends (a person follows the way of their close friend). [Roots Academy](https://rootsacademy.co.uk/blog/how-to-deal-with-doubts-in-islam/?claude-citation-11004a75-a140-4d89-8cd6-334ed2d70a0d=ccf2bde3-461a-41c7-a121-52cb159a79aa)

## 8) A simple dawah flow
1. Greet warmly, build rapport.
2. Ask questions; find the root.
3. Steelman their view.
4. Give ONE clear point with a source.
5. Ask a gentle Socratic question.
6. Make dua for them. Leave the door open. Guidance is Allah's job.
