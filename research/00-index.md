## Simple-English Glossary (read this before anything hard)
- **Atheist** — a person who believes there is no God.
- **Agnostic** — a person who says "I don't know if God exists."
- **Theism** — belief that God exists. **Theist** — a person who believes in God.
- **Naturalism** — the belief that only nature exists; no God, no souls, only matter and physical laws.
- **Materialism** — almost the same: only physical stuff (matter and energy) is real.
- **Premise** — a starting statement in an argument (a reason).
- **Conclusion** — the point the reasons lead to.
- **Valid argument** — the shape is correct: IF the premises are true, the conclusion MUST be true.
- **Sound argument** — valid AND all the premises are actually true. (Sound is stronger than valid.)
- **Fallacy** — a mistake in reasoning that makes an argument break.
- **Cause** — the thing that makes another thing happen or exist.
- **Contingent** — something that COULD have not existed; it depends on something else (like you, a tree, a star).
- **Necessary being** — something that MUST exist and cannot NOT exist, and does not depend on anything. Muslims say this is Allah.
- **Infinite regress** — a chain that never ends going backwards (A made by B, B made by C, forever). Many scholars say a real, endless backward chain is impossible.
- **Epistemology** — the study of how we KNOW things (what counts as proof).
- **Ontology** — the study of what EXISTS (what is real).
- **Objective** — true for everyone, no matter what anyone feels (e.g., 2+2=4).
- **Subjective** — depends on a person's feelings or opinion (e.g., "ice cream is tasty").
- **Steelman** — to say the OTHER side's argument in its strongest, fairest form before you answer it. (Good manners + strong dawah.)
- **Strawman** — the opposite: to twist the other side into a weak, silly version and beat that. (This is a fallacy. Avoid it.)
- **Burden of proof** — the job of giving evidence. Whoever makes a claim has to support it.
- **Theodicy** — an explanation for why a good, powerful God allows suffering.
- **Fitrah** — the natural, inborn feeling every human has that God is real (Quran 30:30).
- **Qadr** — Allah's divine decree; that Allah knows and has measured all things.
- **Kalam** — Islamic theology that uses reason to defend belief; also the name of a famous "the universe began, so it has a cause" argument.
- **Empiricism** — the idea that knowledge comes mainly from what we observe with our senses.
- **Divine command theory** — the idea that things are good/bad because God commands/forbids them.
- **Contingency argument** — a proof for God: contingent things must trace back to a Necessary Being.
